================================================================================
HIT401 Group 34 Capstone Project — Dataset Provenance and Sources
================================================================================

1. "GroundwaterHeads_20250718"
   - Provenance: Shared by project supervisor Dr Cat Kutay on 6 August 2026,
     prepared by Assoc. Prof. Dylan Irvine (Charles Darwin University).
   - Contents: Groundwater level records for 16 unique monitoring bores in the
     Ti Tree Basin, Northern Territory (17 LocationExport folders — bore RN006543
     has two export folders from 2025-07-18).
   - Record count: 60 CSV time-series exports containing 711,627 measurements
     (706,752 Publish logger readings and 4,875 Field Visits manual dip readings)
     spanning 21 February 1967 to 12 December 2024.
   - Variables: "Depth Below Ground (m)" and "Water Elevation (m AHD)".

2. "NT-NaturalResourceMapsBoreData/Bores.csv"
   - Provenance: Provided via email exchange with Assoc. Prof. Dylan Irvine;
     downloadable directly from the Northern Territory Natural Resource Maps portal
     (NR Maps NT: https://nrmaps.nt.gov.au/nrmaps.html).
   - Contents: Complete NT-wide bore register containing 43,099 bore records.
     Attributes include bore number (Bore no), name, depth, yield, completion
     date, static water level, drilling purpose, construction status, coordinates
     (latitude, longitude, UTM easting/northing), and official NTLIS bore report URLs.
   - Data cleaning note: 10 lines in Bores.csv contain stray unescaped quotation
     marks inside bore names (e.g. line 930: 'MCMAHONS 6"CAS 72K E OF 3/WAYS',
     where the inch symbol '"' breaks naive CSV parsers into 19 or 20 columns).
     The webapp cache builder (webapp/build_cache.py) reconstructs and repairs all
     10 occurrences rather than dropping data.

3. "BOM-Datasets"
   - Provenance: Provided by supervisor, sourced from the Australian Bureau of
     Meteorology (BOM) Climate Data Online (https://www.bom.gov.au/climate/data/).
   - Geographic Coverage & Actual Stations:
    The folder contains daily rainfall data (Data.csv) and station notes
     (Note.txt) for 11 BOM weather stations across Australia, including 2 Northern
     Territory stations:
       * 015643: TERRITORY GRAPE FARM (NT) — Lat: -22.45°, Lon: 133.64°, opened 1987.
         Located within the Ti Tree Basin study area (~42 km from central Ti Tree);
         serves as the primary observed rainfall record for Ti Tree climate and recharge models.
       * 014050: FORT HILL WHARF (NT) — Lat: -12.47°, Lon: 130.85°, opened 1882.
         Located at Darwin Harbour; provides Top End tropical rainfall context.
     Plus 9 interstate stations included for comparative climate context:
       * 009021: PERTH AIRPORT (WA) — Lat: -31.93°, Lon: 115.98°
       * 010558: GNOWANGERUP (WA) — Lat: -33.94°, Lon: 118.01°
       * 023055: ADELAIDE AIRPORT ALERT (SA) — Lat: -34.95°, Lon: 138.52°
       * 031049: MULGRAVE MILL (QLD) — Lat: -17.09°, Lon: 145.79°
       * 033307: WOOLSHED (QLD) — Lat: -19.42°, Lon: 146.54°
       * 040913: BRISBANE (QLD) — Lat: -27.48°, Lon: 153.04°
       * 066006: SYDNEY BOTANIC GARDENS (NSW) — Lat: -33.87°, Lon: 151.22°
       * 086282: MELBOURNE AIRPORT (VIC) — Lat: -37.67°, Lon: 144.83°
       * 094029: HOBART (ELLERSLIE ROAD) (TAS) — Lat: -42.89°, Lon: 147.33°

4. "StreamflowData"
   - Provenance: Sourced from the Northern Territory Government Water Data Portal
     (NTG Aquatic Informatics).
   - Contents: Continuous river discharge data for station G0280010 (Woodforde
     River at Arden Soak), the primary gauged river crossing relevant to the
     Ti Tree groundwater basin. Includes daily discharge volume (ML/day) and
     BOM/NT water quality code ratings.

5. "krishna-code/data.zip" (Spatial Layers & Hydrochemistry)
   - Provenance: Packaged by Krishna Dhakal for exploratory geospatial dashboards,
     sourced from the NT Department of Environment, Parks and Water Security GIS layers.
   - Contents:
     * nt_bores/Bores.shp: Spatial shapefile of bores across the NT.
     * nt_bores/Bores_groundwater_level.shp: Monitoring layer containing 2,274 groundwater
       monitoring bore locations and 131 streamflow gauges across the NT.
     * nt_bores/Bores_water_quality.shp: Comprehensive NT-wide water quality dataset
       containing 84,769 chemical samples across 16,929 bores (1909–2021).
     * ti_tree/data/: Ti Tree aquifer spatial layers including basin boundary,
       groundwater salinity, aquifer thickness, depth to groundwater, and water level contours.
